# Screenshots

## Sizes

| Store | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App Store | iPhone 6.9″: 1320 × 2868 portrait (or 1290 × 2796) | Up to 10. Smaller iPhones scale from this set. The app is iPhone-only (`supportsTablet: false`), so no iPad set is needed. |
| Google Play | Phone: 2–8 images, 9:16, 1080 × 1920 recommended | Plus `artwork/play-feature-graphic.png` (1024 × 500) and `artwork/play-icon-512.png`. |

Capture them on a simulator or device from a development build signed into the seeded demo circle. Use real-looking but consenting people: team members or licensed stock, never real users’ photos.

## Shot list

Each shot has a short caption above the device in the brand serif (Georgia Black, `#242117` on `#F6F1E8`).

1. **“One selfie. One shared little world.”** A memory detail screen showing a vivid illustrated group scene.
2. **“Your people, once a day.”** Today: the roll call with 4 of 5 friends checked in and mood emoji.
3. **“Snap it. That’s it.”** Check-in: the front-camera frame, mood chips, and the AI switch visible.
4. **“A new little world every night.”** The Memories grid with 6 to 8 memories and unread dots.
5. **“Keep it. Share it.”** A memory with Save to Photos and the iOS share sheet open.
6. **“Private and invitation-only.”** Circle: the invite code card and members list.
7. **“You’re always in control.”** The report sheet, or Settings with Blocked people, Delete selfies, and Delete account.

## Accuracy rules (both stores)

- Show only features that exist in the build you submit.
- Don’t show the status bar with a carrier name or personal notifications.
- Mark AI imagery as what it is; caption 1 or 4 may say “AI-illustrated”.
